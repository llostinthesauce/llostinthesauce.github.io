about me & this website

nuBlog is the home of my favorite photos, writings, builds and coding projects, plants/garden logging, and misc links. i made this site originally on an old raspberry pi in the spring of 2025, with the goal of it being an old web geocities style site. I wrote it all in .php. while this was fun, it was unstable and my first true dive into linux servers. for reliability, i migrated it to github pages sometime late 2025. now the page has blossomed into my digital home base! if you're reading this, please have a great day!!! :3
