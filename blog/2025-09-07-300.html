300%

Originally published: September 07, 2025

oftentimes if i find myself at a social gathering, such as a party in my college town, i could not feel more out of body and alien. or if i am tagging along with my parents or sisters to some event, i sometimes can get very (anxious), but most of the time i still want to go.

there are two forces inside me. the human side that craves being social, and the introverted/easily overwhelmed side, and they are at odds here. of course it is not all one or the other, as this phenomenon is the human subconscious creating a mental state, which is a consequence of something being uncertain, or me not knowing who/what will be there… at least i think?

I can be so hyper-aware of my surroundings, such as where the thermostat is, how often the door opens, what noise the register makes, or how many times someone cleared their throat. i may have said nothing at all in fifteen minutes of meeting someone, but i remember mostly every word they said, and am still processing all of it at once. i wont remember the clothes they wore or their name, but i will remember what iPhone model they had. i can be running thru ten hypothetical things to say, or things i already said. it all grows so exhausting.

on the contrary, i do not feel this way when i am around comfortable places, or all familiar people. going to class, hanging out with friends, or being by myself, i do not feel this way.

this strong situational awareness can be co-morbid with an out of body experience, ie derealization, and is quite unpleasant. i feel like a hawk watching for any stimuli.

derealization and i are no new companions, in a sense, we are the best of old friends. i have known them since before i could drive!

(derealization is a dissociative symptom in which the world feels off or dreamlike, where the person experiencing it feels like they are observing through glass or fog. it is often triggered by stress, anxiety, or trauma.)

it is like watching a youtube video at .75x speed, so that you can detect every little detail, and each second that passes feels like a low frame rate. other feelings: anxious feeling in my chest, the mind running at 300% speed, and restless. it is not a superpower.

derealization does not equal hyper-awareness as a consequence of being slightly uncomfortable. sometimes yes it does, sometimes no. sometimes I can tolerate these events with excitement and not dread.

it likely depends on how emotionally regulated I am that day and what the specific event or thing even is. months will go by where I seemingly can exist and engage normally. and sometimes it is beyond possible.

i will get invited to functions sometimes and tell myself i want to go, but just cancel last minute or not show up. this can be flaky, but how i feel is that it felt nice to know i was wanted somewhere vs actually going and being exhausted by having to participate socially.

i feel less this way at parties, but almost always at a loud bar or random gathering. harpos is a nightmare and even if i was intoxicated i feel gooberish. i can get this way at concerts too, or like a graduation party or something.

truly, i have come a long way in how persistent and permeating this feeling is. sophomore year of high school, it was CONSTANT. lately, it is only spawned by specific uncertain events.

heh, does anyone else feel like they are spectating themselves “play life” sometimes? xD
