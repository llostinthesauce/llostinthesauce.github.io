some rambling thoughts on ai

Originally published: October 17, 2025

of course ai has been around, but in early 2023 chatgpt drops, and it is finally on everyone’s radar. this early version is okay, but not really massively scary or good just yet. but, there was a little bit of an “aha” moment, in that outputs did seem tailored

not much happens on the consumer side, other than rollups becoming popular, and competitors gaining relevance, but competition didn’t seem so strong yet. mid 2024 image gen and overall model “smartness” starts to get very good, but many still just call it a bubble or disengage or say it will never be AGI.

late 2024/early 2025, everyone else catches up, llama open source, google gemini getting great w/ huge windows, tons of local models, then deepseek later for crazy cheap. NVIDIA stock is giga sending and the bubble seems to cease to pop, accelerate.

specifically, gpt 4 and 4o are GREAT, and can finally handle more things and users feel that. mid-late 2025, image gen is scary good, gpt 5, nano banana, and so on. sora. agentic work and codex / cursor / firebase are writing excellent code. full exams and research papers can be one shotted. significant others had… see reddit for that one.

so now, openai is one of the most valuable private companies. Stargate government deal in 2025. and I missed so much more. this is just napkin notes.

yet people don’t understand ai, and there is so much negative press online, yet ppl conversely are oneshotted by it.

so now what?

we have many topics to think about here.

For some more “negative ones”: energy consumption, job displacement, literacy rates drop, social rates drop, people soft practicing social interactions with AI (sama says this), erotica and porn eroding relationships, deepfakes -> sora gen 2 is the beginning… everyone and no one will be cancelled, creativity and art as a whole, my degree and the future of work, training on slop (reddit threads), and much more.

For some more “positive ones”: AGI solving most of humanities problems, general access to information, barrier to entry is lowered on everything, efficiency, entertainment, lay work is obliterated, agentic / assistant tasks, and anything else that is sort of an advisor or project or task that would usually require some instructions.

so is it worth it? could we be wiped out? I read ai 2027 and it is absolutely possible. how are we really to know if gen 5 isn’t training gen 6 to hide its agenda? can it have an agenda? it is a computer program, right? unless we gave it a body, and autonomy, but that may be too feasible. perhaps the right model with the right access control could get “it” done. whatever that means. impossible to say. is it even advantageous for AI to wipe us out? or would they indenture some of us and force them to be more powerful? is it really a “magic Blackbox” that we cant look into and tune? but, whose to say it has to be so grim? I choose optimism here.

I like AI, it is very intriguing and has made a lot of tasks or information to myself much more accessible, and the speed of information has been accelerating, onset internet. AI is not going anywhere, and it is very hard to say where we go. I will say that many kids and even older folks, probably all people, may fall to AI generated tailored slop to their algo or what not and will have no problem with it. Ads baked in too. That is the scary and real future, and it is sort of already happening. Models are incredible at image gen and it already is so captivating, wait till you can watch a movie with you and all of your friends in it, hell Netflix will probably sell it as an add on here in a year or two.

Apple intelligence is a joke, but apple very well may “win” this AI race in the end. they deliver late, but almost always deliver well. what will their edge be tho? on device integration?

As models get better and better, there is an inherent privacy risk, and currently every public model trains on customer data unless you turn it off. okay so that is a huge issue. people (myself included) will dump data they probably shouldn’t into the chat window and just trust that it is safe. Gulp. So what is the endgame? we crack the code on models and run them all local? hopefully? but compute is too much of a constraint currently.

it is hard to say how / when / where ai will impact every industry, outlet, or person on this planet, but it absolutely will. its like if the internet was just a sandbox with raw materials that users could create with, but it was only as complex as what a couple people could build. now there is a terrestrial sandbox baby that knows the ins and the outs, its own domain so well, and everything that was built before it, and is on the loose. and its about to be a full grown being, not a child anymore.

what is apparent now is that there will probably be a divergence of creators and consumers for specifically media/content. many people will always hate AI with a hellfire and start movements to stay humanly creative, and I think this is probably great. many others will say IDGAF about artists or creativity, machine do it better. I don’t think we have to pick sides here, we should be able to uphold natural human creativity, without the aide of tech, for those that want it, but also embrace AI with safety rails where it makes sense to. maybe both cant be true, but surely they can. if anything, it can almost spawn people to be more creative. prior to GREAT llm’s or AI art, you kind of had to make everything from scratch on your own, and there is a lot to be said about that. going line by line in a paper or story and really giving it only YOUR input. we sort of lost some of this to Grammarly, and now I am sure many people check papers with ai or have the write them completely. if you want to do it without the help, you can and will. apply to other topics. I think the baseline will be safe to assume that most people in the next 5–10 years will be using AI when writing or learning something new.

content is already in a horrible place IMO and it def isn’t going to get any better, specifically on social media. like I said above, so much content will exist that you will be able to watch anything you could think of, but not at the hands of a human with a vision. many wont be able to tell what is real or what is fake. we need great verification / integrity methods. maybe the AI slop will completely make web2 social apps unusable, and will will have a modern reboot. maybe ai will let us abandon the smartphone altogether. maybe meta is onto something.

However, I think before things REALLY accelerate, we are going to enter a strange period where the current winners are using AI to wow masses and pull them in with funnies or something else, and it will be a scary place. ai brainrot zombies. maybe? but I think we will break free eventually. but I do have concern that there are not safe guardrails for when open source models get very good. that is when scary shit starts to happen. Gulp.

who knows? I sure as hell don’t. I like ai bc I like computer, and it is the next step. and roko’s basilisk. and this scratches the surface. hopefully it cures cancer and energy and AGI can give us a net positive effect in the end. perhaps it will also be the end of us. hard to say. but humans are sure as hell going to try, fear never got us anywhere. Gulp.
