→ github.com/llostinthesauce/whisker

whisker is my OS-level voice-to-text tunnel. the iPhone keyboard captures audio, ships it to a private Mac transcription server over LAN or Tailscale, and inserts the cleaned text back into whatever field you were typing in. the phone is just the mic; the Mac owns the models, cleanup, and speed.

server is FastAPI with bearer auth, three parakeet-mlx profiles (fast, balanced, best). default bind is loopback, real use goes over a tailnet IP. still iterating.
