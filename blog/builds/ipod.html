ipod / apple

November 2024 — current

november 2024, i flash modded my iPod 5.5 video with an iFlash SD adapter. genuinely life-changing. the battery lasts DAYS, it's lightning fast, and i have 220~ gb of ALAC 44.1/16bit music on there. pure lossless. love it.

the one issue: lossless files + large album art = the iPod skips on big tracks when it tries to load them into its 64mb cache. everyone says Rockbox fixes it. i resisted for a while, finally caved, installed it, lasted one day. constant headaches just getting the library to work so i went back to stock.

then i gave it a second chance. reinstalled Rockbox, actually committed to it this time, used it for about a month and it was fine until my library started breaking. tracks not loading, sync issues, and i had a roadtrip coming up and needed it to work in the car. switched back to stock again. sigh.

April 2026 — wip. still on stock, still occasional cache skips. i also flash modded a gen 4 iPod right before the roadtrip and am still setting it up.

currently September 2026 — iPod works great stock on iPod os. 256GB micro sd is reliable, and the solution to the low RAM skipping was to find all of the tracks greater than 40 MB on disk (this was my tested cutoff), and compress them to get them right to that 40MB mark, then close the base full res library separately, using symlinks to the OK tracks, and having a folder of the 500~ some tracks that i had to mildly compress. this solved the issue. Damn you KGLW, BC,NR, The Microphones, Pink Floyd, and Cindy Lee for having so many god damn songs that are LONG AS HELL. i love it tho

and on the ipod 4th gen. successfully modded, but the OS is so ass on it. its just a paperweight rn, but i will return to it. the "cache" here is that the RAM is only 32MB. so same issue as above, just an order of magnitude worse.
