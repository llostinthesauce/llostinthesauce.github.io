review: nausea (plus other..)

Originally published: May 15, 2025

Nausea comes at an interesting time for me. It is no bible, and is not perfect by any means, yet the gavel strikes. Sartre has a way with his words such that there are spurts, extraordinary moments of pure genius that I have not encountered elsewhere in writing. Namely in the descriptions of mental anguish, the Nausea, internal monologuing of gruesome interactions, and vast metaphysical (nearly ambiguous) descriptions of objects or the environment. The flow of the novel teeters on the cusps of fluid and manic. Reflective. As mentioned in the forward, it reads like an excerpt out of what could be a larger work. I am happy that it is not a part of a larger work, as this gives it an intimate charm.

One of the largest takeaways is how familiar the subject matter feels. Many of the feelings and means of experiencing them are nothing new to me, yet are described in ways that expand or i have not thought of them. In this sense, Nausea was deeply personal to me, as I have had my serious bouts of existential crises in different forms over the years, and will likely continue to have them. (2015, 2018, 2022). The most shocking of these instances to my being was in 2015: i was incapacitated by many of the themes in this novel. For months i was frozen, unable to find anything, riddled by my existence and lack of impact or place in it. Luckily my parents imposed no strong religious or moral framework onto me, and quite frankly were more laissez-faire in their approach, at least to my understanding. Combine this with a familial history of anxiety and me! And you get an explosion! I overcame my own Nausea in this sense by voiding myself of the internet and spending as much time as possible outdoors. I biked so much that summer, this too is why music truly came on my radar. Something unspoken there.
 
A secondary takeaway is that many things exist in this natural world just as they are. They can be boxed in, explained, defined, studied, and so on, yet this just strips them of their essence and place in the present. This is a big challenge to me, as I have always relied on figuring out the “why” of subjects, purely because it is of interest to me, and in negative light, as a cope from my serious unrest within myself. It is hard to estimate the exact instance that a kernel will pop, but when it does you cannot deny it. The true essence of people and life and subjects is that they appear as they are, to be pushed and pulled on, not to be merely studied and indexed. This goes for ranking, postulating, rumination, and all things overthinking. Taking notes here…

A third takeaway is that meaning in life must come purely from within and is often found in present moments. This can be propelled by attitudes and not seeing barriers, color, stereotypes, definitions, genres, and so on. Many people act to fill their roles and confirm themselves in these roles, I too am guilty of this. Partially it is natural, and human to do such things, yet we are beyond that. Many feel as they do yet do not act on it. Being vs Becoming. The self that is and the self that sees the self that is. There is a distinction here, and many people exist mal-aligned in this. This can be viewed as inauthentic and is why there are silent sufferings or lack of personal meaning behind social structures. If the status and social structures dropped, what do we have?

A fourth takeaway is the importance of authenticity. Everyone has a unique point of view, and we are all one. The importance lies in if one is acting authentically or for some other ends. Every one person has every right to find fulfillment how they so desire, and no one should truly judge. I have been guilty of this. To elaborate, being raised and told that I have to to xyz within school or socially, has made me think this way, less or more of certain careers or paths in life, and I have been straying from this more than ever. College has opened my eyes in this, and I am very thankful. I used to hold the belief that certain majors or activities or careers are superior, and in reflection it was to only uphold some worldview or barrier that I had been taught or created for myself. One has to recognize that happiness is not transitory and the happiest people often are the most present, and what their job is or time is spent doing, does not matter to outsiders looking in. If it is for the authentic reasons, then nothing else matters, and I am happy for them. I want to live my life this way. I have had inklings of these feelings, yet not space to expand, thus I am here. Some of the best insight is from the most absurd and random people and I find great joy in that. Confirmation bias is incredibly real and it takes an active effort to not fall into this trap, this system of thinking and belief. Thank god I am not on TikTok or internet echo-chambers anymore. Bad for the psyche! Value is not to be tied to human beings, even those i disagree with. Also, knowing that there is a sort of charade going on constantly, protects you from jealousy or being pitted against your own belief systems. You are protected in that you are concerned with your own meaning and essence. Not living a falsity for the sake of others.

The sixth main takeaway is that life is inherently meaningless, and absurdity and today rule. Meaning comes from knowing you exist, and living/conscienceness/existing are in a forever propelling cosmic playground of emotion and interaction. I find this to be true, and meaning comes from what you can make of it all. For some this is creativity, as Sartre proposes in the end, for some it is community, and the truth is meaning is infinite and not to be defined. This also is an ever changing thing. Life is to be ever changing, beliefs and actions are to be ever changing, and I prescribe to this. Past me is very much me, but in a sense he is dead. Gone to memory, and the only tangible thing TRULY is this very moment. I like it better this way. Life is fun when there is no worry about the vast future or the crippling past. This does not mean one should abandon either of these, but approach them with authentic attitude to want better and for seizing the days.

The final thought I have is that many things ought not to be explained. They are meant to be lived, and to live is to engage. Strong beliefs only really hold you back, fixating yourself. This is not a way I want to live. It can feel good to argue or stand on a belief, but it is not all that serious sometimes! Over explaining and controlling life pedantically will kill you and is inauthentic in my eyes. No need to feel angry by others when we all are on our own paths. That would come from insecurity, and I do not feel that. Even in this review, I have tried to box in what my thoughts are, and I dislike that yet it is how I know how to get out what I feel. I feel very core to me that the more i try to create, writing or something of the sort, the better I feel in my head day to day. I will stay by this. I find great joy in it and it is for me and me alone. This entire website, is my medium, I accidentally created it and now have been using it to get so much out of me that I was unaware of. Very nice. 
 
 Existence precedes essence, think less not more, definitions are self imposed limits, misalignment kills, stress kills, being mindful and active in your own life TODAY fuels a full life of raw personal experience, and allows you to engage with others in more meaningful ways. society and humankind sets up many traps for us, i am no bear!
