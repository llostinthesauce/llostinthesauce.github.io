reverse-learning & regeneration of convictions

The only fear that I can find myself giving true validity is about the brevity of my old thoughts. (and this isn’t really a fear, more an observation) I used to write so astutely about topics or myself as if I knew better than the next, or perhaps from a sense of entitlement or something. This is scary! Even my first substack-like post on this blog sounded so absolutist and like finance bro twitter fodder. To be fair, I addressed that in these writings, yet I read them now and note how they make me feel. I can even go back to years and find some writings that ring a different bell.

I am in a reverse-learning mindset or something of that sort. I am actively being mindful about my own voice in these mediums, as well as personal notes, journal entries, text messages, school assignments, conversations, and so on. It is a great exercise to see how I feel differently about this. I have the power to suspend myself from past feelings or beliefs that I put into pen. Objectively, subjectively, these change as we age and new events unfold in my life! Partially, many of my feelings have been fear based. I pit my own beliefs or success or definitions of success against each other, onto myself, and others, while saying things that take some seemingly objective high ground or righteousness. This was very work centric or twitter centric, and also inklings of how I heard people in my upbringing speak. I do not know what in me made me look at this differently now, but I do not wish to speak in such absolutes or for some false confidence or for whatever the reason may be.

A “regeneration of conviction” is where i find myself. Some may find their convictions from anecdotal experiences and some may find it from logic and just thinking thru makes sense, but I think in actuality nobody actually sits down and decides where to find their convictions, I know I don’t, maybe I should! I don’t know, this is my first time being alive. The beauty in this however, is that the subconscious is responsible for much of this internal reasoning/processing than I previously thought or experienced.

I can only think so much about what must be felt. I have been saying that less is more, but I must practice. I can only think my way through every possible route / future / past, so many times before I go insane. And I had that sort of realization as of late. I can never work thru feelings by over explaining them to myself. Or excessive rumination, or suppression. Previously I did suppression, and that does not work, it only causes issues. So I face, or am attempting to face what I feel with full force and inaction now. I must not take an action to avoid this feat!

In my thinking too, I do not wish to be so rigid, or so black and white on matters. I honestly do not know what my beliefs exactly are. Sure, I have some core values I could name, but when big changes happen in my life, my beliefs sort of have too, and my baseline is… ? I don’t exactly know what. This is the result of finding much identity in other people, whether it be partner, friends, or family. Naturally, this is an illogical foundation, yet this is what I did/have done for as long as I can remember. Obviously I have my own life essence, but I have to sort of unearth it / find it again.

Thinking rigidly only really spawns from anxieties or negative experiences. When my baseline anxiety drops or a stressful situation is no more, I find myself caring much less about most topics and what my “take” is on them. Come on guys, we all do not need “hot takes”, or “cold takes”, or “takes” at all most of the time. Classifying like this is a dissolution from experience, and saddens me. And I have been quite guilty of this at times too.

presently looking around

I am thirsty for life, and my life is wholeheartedly mine. I live for me, and I let the bugs crawl. I sit on the deck and I let them crawl all over my hands and legs and book and chair. I play with them and I set them down safely. This is how I would want someone to treat me. The carp in the pond, the geese defending their babies, the bats at dusk, deer with their big clueless eyes, and the porch kitties of this neighborhood, all live their own little lives. of course a child could conclude this, but that is the point! Perhaps it is that many adults forget this! Look at the fucking leaves for a second, please, what is the rush!

Living experiences everyday reminds me how personal change is, and how the only person that can truly measure that is you. It is only felt from within. Others can externally validate this in some ways, but they do not know what it is like to be in your brain and body. My two months does not feel like your two months. My agonizing early mornings in my bed are not the same as yours, yet we all share the same time and some of the same space. Perhaps it is that many seek out change, to feel better about themselves or for progress, but stop only at the external validation, and to them, that is what change is. I think maybe I have done this before? Maybe people do this often? Maybe they do not. Someone could get this surface level validation, and still be crumbling on the inside.

I am solely responsible for where I am right now, and every decision I have made in my life has lead me to this point. All of these decisions were based on previous ones, to the best of my abilities at any given time. This river of life will continue to flow until the day I die, snaking in its route, flexing in its depth, and varying in its speed. Any previous advice I have given is/was simply a reflection of my understanding of life at that point. Nothing is bulletproof! It would be cool of some of that stand the test of time, but I do not expect it to. My life is not predestined, and I do believe in free will. I also believe in something larger than life, but I cannot put my finger on it.

My reactions and intuitions are also a culmination of my own experiences thus far, and knowing this allows me to be a bit kinder to myself in moments of serious internal agony. This also helps me understand many other kind humans well. Most people really are just trying their best, to the best of their understanding, and this gives no one person any right to hate or write of any other person, without good personal anecdotal reason at least. Everyone* deserves a chance, no matter the preconceptions.

I am here, now! Let me reframe this: not so much a rebirth, or a ground zero. I very much have created falsities for myself, and have clapped for the wrong reasons, but I am here. There is an inherent shock to my mindset now: perhaps a lens, or really the lack of one. A lens can be a guise and is not real. Do not define this! But do define it, because how else can you break what already exists?

perception is deeply personal

There is a very important aspect of asking questions, that I hope humankind never loses. Asking questions in every regard, inward, outward, to anything that can question. I ask the dogs questions sometimes and don’t exactly get answers, but I can fill in the gaps. Most of the time, lately at least, I can ask myself a question about why I think or feel something, and can peel back some of the layers, but sometimes the root is an interesting one to find. Of course it is great to not go that deep, and to just exist, yet deep feelings warrant some sort of explanation, all personal.

We all deserve presence and peace. External confirmation can be positive given some context, but in times of introspection and personal change (literal/mindset/both), it can pose a threat. To reference the beginning of this again, it must come from within! Confirmation can likely take many forms, and in some way, it feels inhuman to reject confirmation. We look for it because we are social critters! This is a pro and a con. It can take the form of people, being on the internet, hobbies, engaging in what is familiar vs not, and so on. Many will not take the flip side of this coin out of comfortability, and I do not blame them. Everyone falls at a different spot on this spectrum, and I respect every-one persons wish to do as they please, that is autonomy after all!

Expanding on perception, there is a true rawness to truly existing in a moment. To really try to distance yourself from your own mind, memories, thoughts, plans, worries, and so on. In practice, this is what we would call being present, or mindful. But to truly perceive a tangible space, is so exhilarating and exciting and exhausting. You have to really let yourself do it, plunge into it. I am not going to tangent about how hard it is to not be distracted by your phone or 1,000,000 other things vying for your attention, but it truly is harder than ever. Break that barrier, and feel the ground beneath you. I am not talking about meditation, and raw perception is quite the opposite in my experience. This expands beyond just a landscape. That is the most pure form of it.

Ideas about learning, people, cultures, drinking, eating, walking, actives, school, family, macro and micro, big and small, dense or vast, are all just made up by the collective conscience of humankind, as well as my own perceptions on the matter. Ideas make up our perceptions, preconceptions, and in turn actions, conversations, and everything else. You feel a certain way about a certain landscape or type of car or door or plate of food or picture because of your perceptions about them. Your past memories confirm these perceptions. Relationships and careers can confirm these too. This is how people grow deeply unhappy! Because how could the way things are, in their raw form, truly ever mesh with any one persons ideas / perceptions about them! AHHH! We are not meant to be stagnant like that! Yet! Society rewards us when we can box ourselves in and be defined like that! Fucking hell! People fit their roles and forget they can do things. We get schemas about places and items and people and let them be. AHHH! Why! This plays into free will too, as one must feel free to be able to pause / subject themselves to things as they truly are.

This is to the tune of defining it all. Explaining everything. Boxing it in. Filing it away, categorize it, compartmentalize it, understand it fully, and never pull it back out to question it or just see it again, but today, not yesterday. Things are not just as they are, for perpetuity. People are not this way either. Why define yourself as any one thing? Many of these ideas are just that! We know a lot less than we think we know. I do not want to self proclaim myself as anything. I never want to be the exact same thing. Humans are meant for more than picking a lane at age 22 and calling it good forever, god I pray I don’t act the same way I do now in 5, 10, 20, years. I have come to realize that these definitions on all things, mostly only create more pain and limits (at least for me). Limits in them selves are completely self imposed. I will likely disappoint people. I will likely surprise people. I also will be perceived as I am. And as I am is quite different for all. But who does it matter for? Me? For them? I don’t know.

With this perception… idea, and so on…. comes many a judgements. This has been the biggest actionable change in my department. I can acknowledge and admit that I took a much more judgmental approach to topics previously, and I have others to thank for helping me challenge this, as well as my own introspection. Humans will always compare by nature, and depending on who raising you / when / where / who your friends were / what was normal / what your beliefs are, of course you will have some judgements. I do not want to be a judgmental person, and I do find that it is much easier to not be the more time I spend alone, and the more time I spend interacting with random people. There is something about close people or social proximity or being on the internet a lot that factors into some of this, at least for me. I can hope to be better, and I am not perfect. I want to love all people and believe all people have place and spirit and things to share.

In this too, we all ought to live our own lives. I will not live for someone else, and I hope many don’t fall into this trap. I think that maybe one of the most challenging things a 20 something can do is to acknowledge that their have an implicit bias, and that it serves only as a frame of what they thought they knew. Then, throw the frame out the window, and find the new frame or keys to this, based in personal growth, pure perception, and rawness. (Of course a FULL upheaval is extreme, but for the sake of example you should get what I mean)

All of this is a lot, living in this world in 2025. We have so much stimulation and options constantly. You have to engage to some degree, even the less you want to engage. As people are subjected to more media, how does their taste for it change? I have found this to grow in different ways, and it makes sense. The speed of information is fucking insane now, and is out of touch with the speed of real life. A national park spot can blow up on TikTok overnight and now there are no workers or facilities to meet the demands. Plug and play for many things. I’m not sure what the point is, but to be more present, mindful, and really be tapped into your own life, I think you have to get off of the internet and “fake life”. I sound like a broken record at this point, but It does incredible things to the psyche. For me, it is likely more personal than it is for just normal reasons, but the end is the same. Less accessibility, less speed, less attention grabbing, less data, and so on, and so forth.

less is more, trust yourself, proceed with authenticity
