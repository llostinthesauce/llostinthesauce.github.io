perpetuity

Originally published: September 05, 2025

once again, this post is an expansion on ideas from my journal entries or notes from the last week or so. I have many more focused ideas in the cannon, but this is just an amalgamation of recency.

I sit on my balcony in the morning and read. in the morning, day feels so fresh, and I see the rabbits come out for breakfast. conversely, when I sit on the balcony in the evening and read, I see starlings overhead and i remain until the light is too low to continue. this daily ritual is beyond soothing, and I daydream that my future living establishment allows for something similar!

I recently read Anne Frank’s diary, and she wrote that, “paper is patient” and she could not be more correct! I am not so patient with paper however, as I have many burgeoning thoughts. I skip writing for a few days, and before I know it, I could write a full essay on what flows out of me! yet when I put this in practice, my conscience grows quiet and calm, so it is not all for nothing

she also said that, “in its innermost depths, youth is lonelier than old age” and roughly that it is twice as hard for young people to hold their convictions and feelings because they are constantly being shattered and reformed.

I believe that values and essence are perpetual, in that they are things we strive for, but never reach. they can never be perfect, or obtained! there is always a chase!

however, i can critique myself in that I perhaps decide how i feel about some things, or will make a claim or thought that I WANT to believe, but i don’t actually do it in full. yes, as i said above, one cant ever “reach” ideas or values, but they can always strive. i have noticed that it takes much longer after saying/thinking what I want to actually live that way. perhaps it takes time to adjust preferences and ideas, thru repetition or time, that is a survival mechanism for change?

cognitive dissonance is not inherently a bad thing, as it is essentially a contradiction that one is not immediately aware of, and allows for reflection or adaptation.

i have today, and yesterday is not today, as today is not tomorrow.

to me, a well lived life is one full of different experiences, so i ought to try more things more often, always, and almost never say no*, and do as my heart desires, in all aspects.

as I said last post, i am horrified at the memories i have yet to make, because they can torment me, or bring me joy for eternity; the scared child in me says to, “stay inside” and not make new ones… but the hungry human in me says, “those are all past mes, go live sir”

so in living, loss is an inevitable, and no one can calculate how it will be perceived or when it comes. this is terrifying. this is one reason why the future scares me. i am scared for the pain i have not yet felt… the things i don’t even know i could lose yet… But to be paralyzed by that is to not live.

do many people act? does it matter? how many have their existences shaped by factors that they do nothing to change, or perhaps even enjoy. misalignment with the desires in your heart of hearts and your outward actions means that one plays the social or work charade, distracting thru any means, and at the expense of authenticity and human connection.

“an idle mind is the devils playground” means that humans are meant to do, we are meant to create, to love, to cry, to laugh, and so much more. I talk way too much, so I write, and I record.

I also love the idea that i truly do not know much, yet i should be more sure of my words. I have spoken much on convictions, and all of these words are very mutable, but i feel strongly as i write, so i ought to give it space for it to live.

I can write all day but that is not living. I do not think that expression in writing makes me better than another or more nuanced. some may write essays to prove elitism or their intelligence over others. I do not think what I write is inherently profound, and this serves no purpose to impose onto others or ulterior end. i am not very proud of any of this, and sort of feel like the present quality is a bit slop. I write to express and to get out of my head, as well as a sort of log of what I was thinking about. and to share with my family and friends.

there was a Geordie Greep lyric about him loathing a man who “sits in the park and writes his sonnets”, and the subject is painted as a insecure person who values their own image and importance above all, using knowledge, masculinity, vanity, and artistry to put others down. I would like to note that this is NOT me, and if I become like the subject then I am cooked, but I don’t forsee that anytime soon.

I do still feel my youth, and that at 22, my brain is still so malleable and I don’t know what I want. I feel burdened by the possibility, it is overwhelming (in a lovely way!) so much to do, so many paths, so many options, yet one life to fill!

i have experienced so much. i have learned so much. i am beyond grateful for the rich life i have lived thus far, and yet i have learned so little about this world. a misnomer is to feel like I have it all figured out, i don’t. and i do not feel permanently tethered to anything i have done. I don’t want to box meself in, and for that, life does not feel small, it feels large and endless.

To prescribe to one belief or path or way of thinking is to shrink life, i have observed this in others and myself. perhaps life is so much more. and so much less if you make it to be. erase the boundaries of the self, and the possibilities are great, even insane things, insane to our social norms perhaps. why shan’t i become a monk, or learn the way of the sea? the only thing holding me back is the pre-conceived notion that i ought to not, via social conditioning, my parents, or how i think about myself.

I saw online that someone said, “feelings are compressed judgments formed from countless micro-signals we can’t consciously track” AND “feelings are not the enemy of reason, but the precondition of it”. this resonates because it validates that reason and convictions spawn from life compounding. input = output. do nothing and that is all internal, do everything, and there is a diverse map that reason can pull from.

I also recently read Siddartha by Hermann Hesse, and I quite liked the cyclical journey of purpose and experience that he endures.

opinions mean nothing… they could be beautiful, ugly, clever, foolish; anyone can embrace or reject. teachings and religious texts do not reflect opinion… for those who seek to answer the big questions, they reflect salvation and serve a different end for them. one can dismiss heavenly teachings easily, and deem that “enlightenment” comes from within. to those thirsty for knowledge, experience perhaps fills this.

this is about the “you” that persists, your heart of hearts… think lived experience versus studied experience.

Meditation and self isolation/deprivation are not so different from escapism in a modern sense. both distance the self from true/raw experience.

in the end, hate breeds hate, and to loathe someone is to loathe yourself. love breeds love. yet, “humans are beasts, and all they need is permission.”

as humans, we must LIVE! life is uncertain, something either happens or it doesn’t, and most ideas are approximations at best, and only hold us back. there is no correct or incorrect way.
