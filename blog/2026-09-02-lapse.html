lapse

Originally published: September 02, 2026

haha! my first post of this entire year that isnt some shoddy field note, take it for what its worth!
oh, hello now, how some time has passed !
oh where am i, and what comes of now?
oh, open your mouth wide!
a universal side,
for any present party, the ocean blooms, it keeps me alive, and life has been a whirl,
from things beating and never stopping
to the idiosyncrasies

or ironies of man
or the islands of men who weep at the altar
i presume godly men do not impose

and i presume satanly men do
i seek no consequence or blame
or revenge or jealousy
upon any man
the sour souls reap what they ought to
the giants ought to shrink
nine to fifteen long months gone in a blink
what are the fruits of this? if any?
and are people to bear fruit altogether?
some seek this succulent fruit as a means to no definable end
and to that i say, what a shame
what a shame to fill ones days with the fodder of past times or stains

or the good morrow, or
of cognitive dissonance,

running so rampant among those of our ages

of those close

and of those far
and of myself, indeed
what absurd lengths we go to to feel mild comfort in present day

or of tomorrow

and what sadness “things” brings us all

who cannot see what was clear, perhaps by choice !

its an infinite slot machine

of trial and error we endure,

day to day, month to month, and year to year

sometimes the big win comes

after many days gone past

with no one to share it with

or the one we wish to

was with another to share *that with

and chasing something internal and infernal that can never satiate

that feeling that once was

i fear many do this, subbing and swapping

in activities or persons that fill part of the void

but part of that void is never to be filled

and never will be

unless one goes back

which we modernly see as a crass regression

to something that didnt work

in odds of “bettering ourselves” like they say on instagram,
or escaping something “toxic” like they say in therapy

or some other horrible decision imposed by another

or inspired by another,

or motivated by “another”,
as a way to box our lives in and out
as a means to not live
and as a means to justify complacency
or behavior we think today as correct and appropriate
what of the eccentricities,
or what of the compulsions of a place or of a person
what of those magic moments that time has come to swallow whole
and engulf deep down into the belly of the beast
to swell and foster subconscious desires
of the future self
how much of this is necessary? how much is distraction?
saying for certain is a paralysis

and choosing forever is a paralysis,
and those who burn the homes

of who they see, reap little

and late

and elsewhere

a brother who “wouldnt do that”

indeed, did such a thing

in a room of homeless artifacts

who sell some story that isnt theirs

in a town where some version of me

wandered

but i never met that version face to face

though others did

ill keep my sentence

no office can correct that
or window or line or form or clerk to fill in

and those who tried to comfort or exist

are punished by their continual care

and of continual care

of people past

and times past

and places past

one can be punished by memory

not to re-live it

and not of regret

but of retrospect and hind-ness

some sort of clarity that only time can gift

and the everlasting question

of what was that in the first place?

and did it have a second place?

and what to do with it now?

if anything

if nothing, then what of any of it?

of some place, of a large double door

and room i belonged in

(i, or you) waiting for some break in that signal

that i was not strong enough to name

i guess dead air isnt silence

the carrier still lasts beyond;
one had been standing in that room a full year

i kept it clean, tuned, operable

so a voice could go over the full town

but it never really came back to me

some have a yearning for this

something they could never admit

and we see universes in our past

and our eventual futures

and our decisions dont make sense

they rarely do

our day-to-day

i see it

as a way to get to tomorrow

we plan for futures

that never happen

or re work pasts

that didnt fit what we wanted at that time

and i blame no one for this

or myself for this

but i do at the same time

and we would be lying to ourselves to say we didnt

mask our actions with some sort of

glossed explanation

or coating

or covering

or bed to lay it in

to keep cracking on

today

and tomorrow

with some new face

and some new routine

hoping that burning relic

does not keep us up at night

yet it does

and it will

and it might hurt

more than we would ever name

to our new person

but we cant

until someday

it fades into something that feels so distant

and so unfamiliar

that we dont even recognize it anymore

and i wish i had the grace

of hating a person

or burying my thoughts of them

or making soup for them

but i do not get such grace

oh, i envy those who do

and i hope they do not suffer as some do

its easier to keep NOT an idea,

some kitchen i will never learn the sound of

some place i will never knock at

nor in the one i wish for

or the one i see at night

i guess i hope the room is warm

as i know it is

and sometimes i wonder at all

if those people

ever even think

of ones niceties

or recencies

or mistakes

or qualms

or imperfections

and i come to the conclusion that

no such things matter

i cannot know

i should not know

and what does it change

like an orchid that comes again

my mind ebbs and flows in these states

and it balloons and deflates

ever so graciously

but to those

i cant say words today

i wish all the best

i would be lying to say

i didnt think

the cards all

could have fallen

into different roles but

they have not !

make our beds

ill make mine,

i am well

just once i drop my veil

or my sunglasses

of the present

and of september 2026

sometimes,

these ideas come back around

and they burn,

and they cut,

and i wish they didnt,

yet of these ideas,

no home feels cozy for them,

so they end here, maybe

someone reads them

and feels the same in any capacity

i dont ask for a reconnaissance

or a worldly meeting

just a word.

we cannot guarantee much

and what feels so certain

one learns is often

very fleeting

and i havent mulled them so much as i used to

this comes as an epilogue

these lasting thoughts

of lasting people that permeated my being

so i will slide my mind

off of the jagged cliffs of artifact

and return to the now

and jump off ;

that these ideas skirt the edge of

and to you i say:

ask yourself :

what the fly would do

if it had

a few weeks to live, as they do,

what they would say to those they loved

what would they do if silence spoke to them so loud

that no noise could cause a true lapse

what would they do if they returned to a homeostatic base,

where no outside force,

or imposed direction

caused them to change their daily actions or words?

what comes of that?

that is what i would ask,

and of that,

i exist in space

i hope for a good while

but not even that is certain!

my life moves on

all lives move on,

with or without me

and that is what burns the most

one can stop

halt at that

and it all marches on ever so much

a joke, cosmically at that !

i can stare at the stars

the ever-expanding cosmos,

or the meteor shower two weeks ago,

it comes back every august

and knows nothing has changed (since last august, not literally)

because it’s scared of the world

where it is needed.

but it knows a few pennies

could maybe make a difference

on that slot that pays to no one

but it’s just a rock on fire,

so i throw them away,

and i can beat my fists into the Ripe earth

and flagellate internally

or disassociate it all to the world

or have it concern me none at all

which is what takes

its present form

most of the time

these days

but

its my job to figure it out i guess

living in memory

that pangs my stomach

so much

again and again

every season

knowingly, as i do,

in time, in good soil
