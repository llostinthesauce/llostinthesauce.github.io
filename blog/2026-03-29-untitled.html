field notes i: march 18 - march 29

Originally published: March 29, 2026

the following is a short reflection on voice notes, journal entries, scribbles, and conversations amongst the great people of the west

whilst driving across kansas, colorado, utah, and nevada, I would ebb and flow in my motivation and emotional state. sometimes I could not have been more amped to keep trucking, and other times I was stuck deep in thought, re-hashing the past year of my life and how wild it has all panned out. but that is life eh? I do find it hard to not compare previous memories to the present in front of you, alas we are creatures of habit, in both behavior and in thought. honestly some of the sadness comes from the simple fact of being a human male and my circadian cycle. 7-11 am is mostly always great. mid-afternoon I find is when the slump happens, then I pull myself out, either thru thought, or thru genesis of new memories and ideas. when one is in a car alone for 10 hours a day for three days in a row, of course one wants to seek social interaction or starts maladaptive daydreaming inward or whatnot. birthdays are always a little somber, so i figured I would just get out of the house, and for a while. because sitting in my hometown and just hanging with my buddy claude code and applying for jobs all over the country got pretty dull after 2~ months. of course, I love my family and the hobbies I have picked up, but there is some massive crossroad that lay ahead. stay in my hometown and begin real life there, where it is familiar and safe, or say f it and go out. I've been wanting to "go out" for many years now, and I sorta am getting a real taste of it now. part of me up and leaving may perhaps be running away from my soured internal final month or so at school, or the undesired drama and fixation that consumed a lot of my brain power in the last 6-9~ months. but also all we can do is move forward duhhhh. so here I am. generally, I am happy, and time marches on

traveling alone is incredibly different that with people, and with that, a road trip alone is a different feat. this is not business travel or a quick weekend trip, the scope here was intentionally open ended and free. VERY different vibes every day. hey so I am tired? okay don't do anything. I want to sleep in? ok. I wanna sleep in the car in my parents california home town? sure. i choose what I eat, drink, and do when i want 100% of the time. something about freedom of choice or illusion of control and personal power here, but it is indeed gratifying and fulfilling. every action in a foreign place has to come from me and really me only. it is all a choice. i love it

I feel like I almost actually didn't go to university. when i was driving through Denver i remembered visiting the first chipotle ever, which is located right on the edge of a campus there, and i recall how many students were hacking around and having fun. as this memory came to me, i thought, "wow that sounds so fun to be a college student," completely forgetting the fact that I was a full-time college student for what… five years… I don't really know how that flipped in my head, but I guess… Something about my college experience and how it just was, I don't know a blur between all of the factors at play, switching my major, community college, and living on / off campus and coming home each summer. i guess the takeaway is that it did not feel much like a congruous experience. in my mind, college is a very fragmented moment in my past that i do not miss or seek to relive. but it also would have been nice to have been 18 and gone out of state in a far away place and did ALL of the normal college things. I sorta got a late start and edge case for my experience but i wouldn't change a thing.

all of the billboards in Provo, Utah are pertaining to: missionary work, missionary checking accounts, savings accounts, energy drinks that don't have any caffeine, plastic surgery, tummy tuck, weight loss, and then just injury lawyers and stuff like that. insane place. is the arsenic ppm correlated here? but hey costa vida. also, all of the drivers here are just good? maybe? but frick driving the interstate in rush hour. that was horrible.

ghost towns are wild. gold rushing would have been fun af. i even have levis and I've put my time in on Minecraft to know a little bit of ball. ;)

getting to see a newer friend from last years bcnr concert, ella, was great! i love connecting with people all over and it makes me happy and hopeful to continue building a friend network all across the country and globe. Logan, Utah is goofy but very sterile in a comforting way and a bit quaint. i just can't actually imagine living there. but the thai food was gas. and chopping it up in the graveyard was necessary.

mandatory stop at my diamond in n out in vegas, however it was not that great this time. nostalgia ruins it again. tbf, the in n outs in la county clear the vegas or sd ones by a long shot. but its all the same crap, right? different pile perhaps.

also driving into california from vegas, i encountered another sobering realization. driving thru the mojave with quadeca playing, i totally zoned in to out of thought. i thought how strange it is that I perceived california mostly thru tech companies in my youth, and even still, i think of it this way. but at 23, now i can see that that is merely one way to look at it, and of course my experiences here in the last three years have shifted that dramatically. but truly, i associated california with apple computer, google, and more recently, all of the frontier labs. 50 years ago none of that existed, and if i was raised any time sooner i of course would have had a very different perception of it all. but its just funny, and makes sense, that as a young person in middle america with a father in tech, that i was captivated by these mega-companies worth trillions. it is a little sad, because there is SO MUCH MORE to it, and to any place for that matter. so i have these thoughts as I'm crossing one of the most jarring geographical landscape shifts i could ever perceive and it reminds me how young i am and how new-age of a way to think of a place my previous youth mindset was. i can't even be mad, its just how i grew up. no better or worse than anyone else's, and nobody is taught anything of that sort as a child, it is just where they gravitate and what they absorb. now and for some time i see that everywhere, and everyone is permissible in their own right. sadly a lot of parents probably don't teach their children that. if anything, in america, they are taught more negative connotations about other places. and then, i realize that it is a miracle of engineering to talk to a slab of glass and metal as I'm driving and it just blows my mind. the more time i spend on the computer or hanging with my buddy claude, the more detached you can get from the tangible; this is great and horrible. being inside with claude and then up and driving 2000 miles could not be more of a 180, and i embrace it heavily. wonderful beautiful landscape. creatures of habit and routine paralyze, but we are programmed that way. i feel both sides like a burning passion, the desire to stay home, lock in, and be close to family. and also the desire to escape and do anything else. this trip is a case study in desire and challenge. i stop frequently, i take strange routes, i talk to as many people as possible, and like i said above, perhaps about control in some subconscious way. last minute bookings. and the genius plan to meet my long time internet friends from 2017. all of the brew for a wonderful trip. i too am amazed at highways, interstates, freeways, and the fact the we as humans pooled our collective knowledge together to create tools and machines to traverse and explore these lands easily and comfortably. we take it for granted. one one hand we are set via DNA to stay local but if that was the case, why do we all explore and travel? well we all don't, but. of course we are to evolve, but tech and invention and the collective mind and knowledge has RAPIDLY outpaced human evolution. it may take thousands of years for us to really catch up. no wonder we are all depressed or overstimulated. heh. 35,000~ years is a VERY long time. hey shoutout neuroplasticity tho. we are made to learn and do more always, we just have to foster it. foster obsolescence and stickiness and zed happens. i do look forward to meeting dj and sara, and hopefully other new friends along the way. i have no clue how long i will be out west. maybe a week, maybe a month. maybe all of this is a strive for purpose or a lesson or just experience. all and none are probably true. just different existence i suppose. also absolutely a cop out for not having a job yet, but the search has just seriously started recently. i saw only ONE cop in california on the entire drive in. Nevada had SO MANY. i guess at age 23 i feel more reborn and capable than ever. the last few months have been a little funky, and the last year even funkier, but i feel ready for the now and the future. and i embrace it with open arms. i have mental breakthroughs or just thoughts or genesis of ideas that feel very fresh and like i am heading somewhere great. i have felt this way for many years, but it is always a nice feeling when i consciously feel it vs just subconsciously. my curiosity and aptitude in certain areas makes me feel GREAT! sure i am very nostalgic all of the time too, but i have to take it with a grain of salt. ill be 26 in three years. gulp. half of a life ago i was in 6th grade. Gulp. maybe Chicago is it. maybe it's la. san diego, or sf. i like it all. hell, maybe even salt lake. i still have never been to the east coast. i ought NOT to get thought paralysis. proof in the pudding is that i am here now. i would rather be here, now. mojave has a strange fog or mist as this sunsets. i process very slowly and i have never had a REAL job, that doesn't mean i haven't made real experience or money :demon emoji: ty nike and sony. future core, be kind to yourself. you are gonna have great days and bad days and that is life my boy. human emotions aren't supposed to make sense. just feel em, don't think about em too hard. hey the elevation dropped 2000 ft since i started this note, how dramatic! hey hopefully in 2, 5, 10, 20 years you can look back at this note and think what an idiot. or wow, what a genius! or forget about it altogether.
